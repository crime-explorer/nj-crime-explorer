Methodology & Data Notes

  • Statewide Estimates (2021–2025): Derived from FBI Uniform Crime Reporting (UCR) Summary Reporting System estimates, which impute missing agency months (2025 NJ average monthly population coverage was 98%).
  • Offense Definitions: Violent crime comprises murder/nonnegligent manslaughter, rape, robbery, and aggravated assault. Property crime comprises burglary, larceny-theft, and motor vehicle theft.
  • County Rates: Reflect FBI Crime Data Explorer per-agency 5-year averages (2020–2024) across all 21 New Jersey counties.
